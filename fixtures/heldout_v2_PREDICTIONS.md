# Held-out v2: predictions, written before any measurement

Committed together with `fixtures/heldout_v2.json` and before any backend was scored on
it. At the time of writing, the two NLI fixes these predictions concern did not exist in
code. The commit history is the evidence for that order.

## Why v2 exists

Two fixes to the NLI backend were designed after seeing it fail on the stress set and the
v1 sample: decontextualizing premises, and gating contradictions on topical overlap. A fix
designed after looking at a set's failures cannot be fairly scored on that set. v2 is
generated by the same mechanical rule as v1, at an offset that makes every tracing sentence
disjoint from v1, plus 8 sentences from an unrelated page (Python's zipfile docs) whose
truth is `unrelated`. v1 contained no unrelated references at all, so it could not measure
the one failure the contradiction gate is meant to fix.

## Composition

28 references: 12 verbatim tracing sentences (truth `reinforcing`), 8 negated twins (truth
`conflicting`), 8 unrelated zipfile sentences (truth `unrelated`).

## Predictions

**`rules`, unchanged.** Most verbatim tracing sentences come back `unrepresentable`, as 17
of 17 did on v1. The zipfile sentences come back `unrelated`, correctly, because their
subjects are absent from the page. Predicted: **8 to 11 of 28**, with most of the correct
answers being the unrelated ones.

**`nli-material`, unfixed.** Strong on the verbatim and negated tracing sentences. Invents
conflicts on some of the zipfile sentences, as it did on the Kubernetes reference.
Predicted: **16 to 20 of 28**, with **at least 3 false conflicts among the 8 unrelated**.

**`nli-material`, with both fixes.** The gate removes most or all false conflicts on the
unrelated sentences, turning them into correct declines. Predicted: **21 to 25 of 28**, and
**0 or 1 false conflicts** among the unrelated.

**The risk I expect to see.** The gate strips context words and polarity words before
measuring overlap. For a short negated sentence, very little may be left, and the gate
could suppress a true conflict. Predicted cost: **0 to 2 true conflicts lost** among the 8
negated sentences.

**Reference A.** With decontextualized premises, the pilot's Reference A should come back
`reinforcing` under the NLI backend, where it currently comes back `conflicting`.

If a result lands outside these ranges, that is reported as a surprise rather than
explained away.
